About me

Drawing since elementary school

“During my school years, at the beginning, it was a challenge. But it has turned into a skill, some knowledge, and my inspiration.”

Anja lives in Michigan. Art has been a hobby since her first drawing in elementary school, through school projects and college, and it has become an anti-stress, a therapy, and a way of telling stories without words.
